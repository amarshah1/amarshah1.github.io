Mellon University](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/). I am very fortunate to be advised by
[Marijn Heule](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~mheule/) and [Bryan
Parno](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/user/bparno/).

I work at the intersection of <Term def="the art of automatically deriving logical
conclusions from premises">automated reasoning</Term> and <Term
def="the science of proving the correctness of a theorem or program in a
computer system">formal verification</Term>. My current research is on

program verification. During my PhD, I have been grateful to have spent some
time at the [Amazon Automated Reasoning
Group](https://www.amazon.science/research-areas/automated-reasoning), mentored
by [Bruno Dutertre](https://brunodutertre.github.io/).

Su](https://people.inf.ethz.ch/suz/)'s group at [ETH
Zürich](https://ethz.ch/en.html). Before that, I finished a very joyous
undergraduate at the [University of California,
Berkeley](https://www.berkeley.edu/) studying Math, Computer Science, and
[Logic](https://guide.berkeley.edu/undergraduate/degree-programs/logic/), where
